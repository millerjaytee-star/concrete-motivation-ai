# Concrete Motivation — YouTube Studio Production Package

## Purpose

This document is the owner-ready source for rebuilding the real Concrete Motivation YouTube channel so it matches the website, app, and operating system.

The approved visual direction is cinematic black, charcoal, distressed concrete, white, and construction gold. It is founder-led, family-centered, disciplined, faith-aware, and community-driven.

## Truth and publishing rules

- Never display invented subscriber totals, view totals, video totals, verification badges, testimonials, partners, sponsors, or results.
- The generated channel-page artwork is a visual concept only. Its sample metrics must not be published.
- Use real public URLs only after they have been confirmed.
- Use real photographs of Jaytee and family only with owner approval. Generated visuals must not be presented as documentary photography.
- Do not imitate the voice, slogans, or signature style of a living motivational speaker.

## Banner production brief

### Canvas

- 2560 × 1440 pixels
- Keep all essential text and faces inside the centered 1546 × 423 safe area
- Export as high-quality PNG or JPG under YouTube’s upload limit

### Required banner content

- Concrete Motivation name and official CM mark
- “Build from pressure. Lead with purpose. Move with discipline.”
- Leadership · Discipline · Faith · Family · Growth · Legacy
- “One movement. One mission. One nation.”
- Founder-led visual on the left or outside edge
- Family/community visual on the opposite side
- Black city-at-night or stage atmosphere

### Do not place on the banner

- Subscriber counts
- View counts
- Verification symbols
- Fake social proof
- URLs that have not been confirmed
- More than one primary call to action

## Title rules

- Promise a clear outcome, tension, question, or transformation.
- Use direct language a real person would say.
- Keep most titles under 65 characters.
- Do not use false urgency, fabricated results, or misleading claims.
- Use the brand language only when it improves clarity.

Examples:

- Discipline Today. Freedom Tomorrow.
- Pressure Builds You—or Breaks You
- Leadership Before the Title
- The Provider’s Pressure
- Stop Building Alone
- What to Do After Motivation Leaves
- More Than an Athlete
- Build the Standard Before You Need It

## Thumbnail rules

- One main face or subject
- Three to six words
- High contrast black, white, and construction gold
- One emotional idea per thumbnail
- No invented numbers, badges, arrows, or fake screenshots
- Mobile readability test at approximately 168 pixels wide
