## Production corrections required

The YouTube channel concept contains illustrative subscriber, video, and view totals. Those values are not verified and must be removed from all production artwork and channel configuration.

The concept also resembles a complete YouTube interface. The actual production deliverables are the banner, profile mark, thumbnails, channel copy, playlists, trailer, links, and homepage ordering—not a screenshot uploaded as the banner.
